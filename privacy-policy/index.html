Privacy Policy

How Sifcut for Android and the Sifsci website handle your information.

Last updated November 15, 2026

On this page

Data Sifcut accesses

Sifcut is an Android video editor developed under the Sifsci name. When you select media through Android’s file picker, Sifcut reads the files you choose so you can preview and edit them. The app may store project names, edit settings, references to selected files and media details such as duration or resolution.

Sifcut processes your projects and media on your device. It does not send your imported media, project data or exported videos to Sifsci or to our servers. This version of Sifcut does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

File access and sharing

Android controls access to the files you select. On some older Android versions, Sifcut may request storage permission to save an exported video to shared storage. If you open a file from a cloud provider or use Android to share a video, that provider or recipient handles the file under its own terms and privacy practices.

Google Play handles app distribution under Google’s Privacy Policy. Android may back up or transfer eligible app data according to your device settings, independently of Sifcut.

Website and support

The Sifsci website has no analytics scripts, tracking cookies or contact form of its own. Its hosting provider may process technical request information, such as your IP address and the page you request, to deliver, secure and maintain the site.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you include. We use them to respond to your request and resolve support or privacy questions. Email and hosting providers may process information to deliver those services. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law.

Retention and deletion

Sifcut keeps app data on your device while you use it. You can clear Sifcut’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app to remove local app data. Original media and videos saved to shared storage or other apps may remain and can be deleted separately. Device backups can be managed through Android or your backup provider.

We retain support emails for as long as needed to respond, resolve the issue or meet applicable obligations. You can ask us to access, correct or delete information you sent to us using the email below.

Privacy contact and updates

For questions or requests about this policy, email ronit@sifsci.com. We’ll post updates on this page with a revised date when our practices change.
